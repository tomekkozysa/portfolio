## Privacy & Cookie Policy

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and understand how the site is used.


### What We Collect

We use the following third-party services to help us understand how our site is used, improve user experience, or provide functionality:

#### Google Analytics

We use **Google Analytics**, a web analytics service provided by Google Inc. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information such as:

- Pages visited  
- Time spent on the site  
- Browser and device type  
- General location (e.g. country)  

This data helps us understand how our site is performing and how we can improve the user experience.

No personally identifiable information is collected or stored by us via Google Analytics.

[View Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)



### Why We Collect It

We collect this information **with your consent** to:

- Understand website traffic and usage patterns  
- Improve the design, content, and performance of the site  

We do **not** use this information for advertising or profiling purposes.


### Your Choices

When you first visit the site, you'll be asked whether you consent to the use of analytics cookies.

You can:

- Accept or reject cookies using the banner  
- Change or withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or using the “Change Cookie Settings” option available on this site  
- Manage your Mailchimp subscription via the unsubscribe link in any email  


### How Long We Store Data

Cookies used by Google Analytics and other services may persist for up to 2 years. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.


### Third-Party Data Transfers

Some services we use (such as Google Analytics, Calendly, and Mailchimp) may transfer your data outside the EU or UK. These services rely on approved data transfer mechanisms such as the **EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework** or **Standard Contractual Clauses**.


### Your Rights

Under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and the **UK GDPR**, you have the right to:

- Access your personal data  
- Correct any inaccurate information  
- Request deletion of your data  
- Object to certain types of processing  
- Withdraw your consent at any time  
- Request a copy of your data in a portable format  

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@kozysa.me
